# Storyworld Workbench: Pitch

**One-liner:** A writing desk for novelists that remembers your story world and tells you when you break your own rules.

## Elevator pitch (about 30 seconds)

Novelists with big casts and long timelines lose track: a character's eyes change color, someone is in two cities on the same night, a locked door opens with no key. Storyworld Workbench catches this as you write. You mark passages with the people, places, times, objects and rules they involve. Each one builds a living dossier, with traits like "can't swim" or "sealed after the Flood" that hold for a set stretch of story time. A built-in checker and Claude then flag contradictions, suggest payoffs for setups you haven't used, and answer questions like "who knows about the letter by chapter 10?" Photos are described and become part of the canon too. When the book is done, one click turns the whole world into a series bible that showrunners can work from: characters, locations, timeline and a scene breakdown.

**Closing line:** Write the book. Get the bible for free.
